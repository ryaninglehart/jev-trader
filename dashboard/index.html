Study rules and evidence limits

Signals and all six scenarios are frozen before collection. Queue depth consumes actual opposing trade volume; fills can be partial and cannot occur on the decision or inclusion block. Time exits use recorded bid depth after an execution delay. Small partial positions that cannot meet the observed minimum remain unresolved. No daily reset of losses. Outcomes from different scenarios overlap and are not independent samples. A positive result in this exploratory study requires a new, untouched validation period.

How this comparison works

Selective Entry requires 3 seconds of sustained signals, at least 3 opposing trade prints, and trailing trade volume at least twice queue depth plus order size. Buy attempts are spaced 15 minutes apart. Eligible resting quotes keep their original price, with the original expiry and safety checks. Higher fill rates can come with worse prices or adverse selection; zero trades is not evidence of improvement. Both strategies use simulated queue fills, full transaction costs and estimated liquidation. No automatic promotion or live trading.

Strategy, execution and validation rules

Completed 5-minute bars identify an hour-long uptrend, a preceding pullback and recovery with positive taker flow. A frozen training cohort estimates a two-hour price move. Minimum 20 mature examples spaced at least two hours apart; all training labels finish before the test period. Insufficient support credits zero directional edge. Immediate orders are simulated after the decision block, filling completely within the price limit or failing with full gas charged. Entry price allowance: 10 bps. Fees: at least 5 bps per side; entry gate reserves three full gas transactions, adverse selection, uncertainty and minimum profit. One position; 200 MON order size; at most two entry attempts per rolling 24 hours. Trade stop, trend reversal, portfolio stops or two-hour holding limit trigger delayed exits. Incomplete exits stay unresolved.
